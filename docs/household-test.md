# The household test

Decision 12: the web app does not count as done until a household member
who did not build it uses it without help. This is the kit: set up, hand
over the task cards, watch, write down, report.

## Before the test

1. CalZone 0.5.0 runs on the VPS (`deploy/README.md`), with two-factor
   set up for both of you.
2. Your real calendars and contacts are copied from Baikal
   (`docs/leaving-baikal.md`). A test on real data finds real problems;
   an empty calendar finds none.
3. The family calendar is shared between you both with "can edit".
   Each of you has at least one calendar of your own.
4. Her language set the way she wants it (Preferences), on the device she
   will really use: her phone's browser, her work laptop, or both. If
   both, do the phone first.
5. Her phone stays on DAVx5 and Fossify. Point DAVx5 at CalZone first if
   it still points at Baikal (task 10 needs it).

## How to watch

- Hand over one card at a time. Say nothing else, and don't point.
- Write down where she looks first, where she hesitates, and what she
  says out loud ("where is...", "huh"). Her words are the best data.
- **Stuck rule:** after 3 minutes without progress, help with the
  smallest hint that works, and mark the task "helped".
- Mark each task: **alone**, **hesitated** (got there, but searched or
  backtracked) or **helped**.
- Afterwards, ask: "What was annoying?" and "What would you do in a
  calendar that you couldn't do here?"

## The task cards (hand these over)

Goals, not steps; the wording leaves the buttons for her to find.

1. Wat staat er deze week op de planning? Wanneer is de eerstvolgende
   afspraak met (iets dat echt in de agenda staat)?
2. Zet "Eten bij oma" in de gezinsagenda, zaterdag van 18:00 tot 21:00.
3. Het eten bij oma wordt zondag, zelfde tijd. Pas het aan.
4. Zet "Zwemles" in de agenda: elke woensdag om 16:00, een uur, tot de
   zomervakantie.
5. Volgende week gaat de zwemles niet door. Haal alleen die ene weg.
6. Je wilt een uur van tevoren herinnerd worden aan een afspraak in de
   agenda van je partner. Regel dat, alleen voor jezelf.
7. Verwijder het eten bij oma, en haal het daarna terug.
8. Zoek het telefoonnummer van (iemand uit de contacten). Voeg daarna een
   nieuw contact toe met een naam en een telefoonnummer.
9. Geef je eigen agenda een andere kleur.
10. Kijk op je telefoon (Fossify) of de zwemles daar ook staat.

English, for you: (1) what's on this week, find a real upcoming event;
(2) add dinner at grandma's, Saturday 18:00 to 21:00, family calendar;
(3) move it to Sunday; (4) a weekly swimming lesson until the summer
holiday; (5) skip only next week's; (6) a reminder for herself only on
your calendar; (7) delete and undo; (8) find a phone number, add a
contact; (9) recolor her calendar; (10) the series shows on her phone.

Afterwards, check two things she can't see: task 6's reminder does not
ring on *your* phone, and task 10 arrived without a manual sync (note
how long it took).

## Report back

Per task: the mark (alone, hesitated, helped), what she did, and what she
said. Screenshots help; crop or blur real event titles and names, because
I don't need the content, only what happened. Add her answers to the two
closing questions. Everything she trips over becomes a fix, and the
phase is done when a rerun of the stuck tasks goes without help.
